Floos background pictures
=========================

This folder is empty on purpose. Floos ships no images.

Drop a .png in here and it becomes selectable in the config window under
Appearance -> Background Picture. It is drawn behind the panel, dimmed,
clipped to the window, anchored to the right so it does not sit under the
numbers on the left.


Making one that looks good
--------------------------

  * Portrait orientation. It is anchored right and sized to the panel
    height, so a tall image fits the way you expect. A wide landscape shot
    gets cropped to its middle.

  * Dark or high-contrast subjects work best. The picture is drawn at 12%
    by default and a dark scrim is laid over the left side, so anything
    pale and low-contrast simply disappears.

  * Transparent PNGs are fine, and often better - a character on
    transparency with no background of its own reads much more cleanly than
    a full scene.

  * A rough target is 512 x 1024. Bigger is not sharper here; the panel is
    a few hundred pixels tall.


The fade is capped
------------------

The slider stops at 0.30 and the addon enforces it. Above that no amount
of scrim keeps the numbers readable, and an unreadable tracker is not a
tracker. If you want the art louder than that, you want a wallpaper.


Nothing is bundled, and why
---------------------------

Same rule as sounds/. A picture of a Final Fantasy XI character, job
artwork, or a piece of the game's UI is derived from Square Enix's
designs - whether it was drawn by hand, rendered from the game, or
generated by an AI from a description of it. It is fine on your own
machine. It is not ours to put in a public download, so Floos ships none
and neither should a fork you publish.
